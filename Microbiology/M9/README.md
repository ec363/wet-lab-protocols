# M9

M9 media is a well defined media for microbial assays in synthetic biology, in which the components are known and individually titratable, unlike more traditional media like LB or 2TY which are made up of crude lysates of various mixtures and in which the only thing you typically titrate is the salt content. M9 is typically preferred for applications in which:

- the media needs to be more well defined for reproducibility reasons
- you want to specifically remove certain amino acids for auxotrophy studies
- to study the production of low level/low brightness fluorescence in cell growth timecourses (as M9 is more transparent than LB or 2TY).

#### Recipe

Make up stock solutions and sterilise and store as indicated. ('FSd' stands for filter sterilised. RT = room temp.) 

Notes:

- Standard recipe here uses 0.8% fructose – this can be titrated or replaced with other carbon sources.
- Thiamine is an additive specifically required by DH10B cells, and may not be required for other strains.

|                                  | How to make and store stock                                                           | For 250ml | For 500ml |     Final |
|----------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------:|----------:|----------:|
| mQ water                         | Autoclaved, RT                                                                        | 173.75 ml |  347.5 ml |           |
| M9 5X salts                      | Autoclaved,  RT                                                                       |     50 ml |    100 ml |        1X |
| Casamino acids 10%               | Autoclaved,  RT (Make up 50ml at a time, stable for months.)                          |      5 ml |     10 ml |      0.2% |
| Fructose, 20%                    | FSd, fridge (Make up 50ml at a time, stable for weeks.)                               |     10 ml |     20 ml |      0.8% |
| Thiamine hydrochloride (10mg/ml) | FSd, RT, cover in foil (light sensitive) (Make up 50ml at a time, stable for months.) |   6.25 ml |   12.5 ml | 0.25mg/ml |
| MgSO4 0.1M                       | FSd, RT (Make up 50ml at a time, stable for months.)                                  |      5 ml |     10 ml |      2 mM |
| CaCl2 0.1M                       | FSd, RT (Make up 50ml at a time, my latest lasted several years.)                     |    250 µl |   500 µl  |     100uM |

#### Protocol

- At least a day before requiring M9, prepare the constituent stocks (above) as some need autoclaving / cooling before use. Others need filter sterilising with syringes/filters which frequently run out exactly when you need them.
-	Assembly of M9: Assemble in 250/500ml filter bottle under flame to keep stocks sterile. Filter sterilise with vacuum pump. **I tend to assemble it without CaCl2 as it can precipitate, then when I need to use M9, I remove 30ml with a stripette and add 30µl CaCl2 and vortex.** (https://www.researchgate.net/post/Crystals_precipitating_out_of_M9_medium)
- Usage: Only open under a flame to keep stock sterile.
- Storage: M9 1X is light sensitive due to thiamine, so cover with foil. Room temperature storage is fine, and makes bacterial culture preparation easier as you don't need to warm it to room temperature before use. I would suggest a shelf life of 2 months max.
